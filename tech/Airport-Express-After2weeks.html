Apple Airport Express After 2 Weeks

18 of June 2025 - Gamerabbit

After using the Apple Airport Express for two weeks, it has fulfilled my needs. Its main use is for streaming music, which has been great. I can stream from all devices in my home (AirPlay or from Apple Music on Windows) and the sound quality is pretty good!

Not to forget, it also brought internet to my PowerMac, which is way easier than previously having to plug the ethernet into another computer and share the connection.

For the price I got it, I am extremely happy with it. It did everything I expected and I also learned many things from the Airport (characteristics and story...)
