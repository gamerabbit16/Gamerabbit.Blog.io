Airport Express 802.11n 1st Gen

The Airport Express 802.11n came out in 2008.

I received a few days ago an Apple Airport Express 802.11n, which I bought online, and after some negotiation, I got it for 4 euros. Few days after buying it, my Airport arrived. I factory reset it and completed the whole Airport configuration on my iPhone.
Which surprised me a lot, for an Apple product made back in 2008, the integration in the IOS's settings is clean. The little downside is the AirPort App; it feels like it was meant for the older iPhones without a notch, and in-app navigation feels strange, but I think it was designed for users with multiple Airport devices.
