Airport Express 802.11n 1st Gen

The Airport Express 802.11n came out in 2008. I recived a few days ago an Apple Aiport Express 802.11n, I bought online and after some negotiation I got it for 4 euros. Few Days after bying it my Airport arriverd, I factory reset it and did the whole Airport configuratio on my Iphone. Which suprised me a lot, for an Apple product made back in 2008, the integration in the IOS's settings is clean. The little down side is the AirPort App, it feel like it was meant for the older iphones without a notch and in app navigation seems strange, but I think it was made for users that had way more Airport devices.
